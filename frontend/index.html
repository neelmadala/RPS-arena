⚡ Quick play

Approve once, then play with no wallet popups. A temporary key in this browser plays for you from a game balance, and reveals your moves automatically.

The key can only stake from your game balance, and only until it expires. It can never withdraw: only your wallet can. The gas MON goes to the key and is sent back to you when you end the session.
